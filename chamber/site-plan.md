# Chamber of Commerce Project — Site Plan

## Description

A *Chamber of Commerce* is a local organization of businesses that work together to strengthen the business interests of the group.

## Site Name

Name: **Ankadikely Chamber of Commerce**

## Site Purpose

This website will host the overall of the **chamber of commerce of Ankadikely**. It will focus on showcasing businesses from Ankadikely and
share news about the events, fundraising and businesses promotions of the city. It aims at fostering networking between businesses, provides
benefits to members and cultivate relationship. The main goal is to highlight businesses in Ankadikely.

## Target market

Small and big businesses of *Ankadikely*.

## Site Goals

- Suggest and highlight local businesses of *Ankadikely*
- Show benefits as a business of being a member of the chamber
- Enumerate events, fundraising, community aids and other activities
- Promote relationship and networking between businesses

## User Personas

## Scenarios

- A local business owner is interested in joining the chamber of commerce to network with other business owners. They visit the website to find information on membership benefits, fees, and how to apply.
- A community member is looking for upcoming events and workshops hosted by the chamber. They visit the website to browse the events calendar and register for interesting activities.
- A visitor from out of town is considering relocating to the area and wants to learn more about the local business environment. They explore the chamber's website for information on existing businesses, availability of skilled labor, and quality of life in the area.
- A foreigner is visiting the town and want to know about the commerce there. They visit the chamber of commerce site to learn about the surrounding businesses and locate them.
