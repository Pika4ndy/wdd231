About Eureka

Eureka is a science resource hub made for learning. We bring physics ideas and study resources together to help learners explore how the world works.

Our purpose

Science becomes easier to explore when ideas are organized and explained clearly. Eureka is designed to support that journey, from reviewing an equation to building a stronger understanding of the concept behind it.

Explore physics

The resource hub highlights subjects including mechanics, thermodynamics, waves, electromagnetism, optics, and modern physics. Use the topic guides to find a place to begin or continue studying.

Learn with context

Equations are more useful when their symbols, units, and meaning are understood. Eureka aims to connect formulas with clear explanations and examples of how scientific ideas are applied.
