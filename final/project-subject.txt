Project Subject:
    Science Student Resource Hub

Project Description:
    A website where visitors can look for science formulas, science cheat sheet, explanations,
    science topics, science's history, exercises + solutions, subject use cases, examples, quizzes, ... quickly
    and in one single place. It will allows visitors to connect and discuss with other people
    to either chat, collaborate, solves problems, publish contents, ... they can also save formulas,
    track topics studied, save quiz results, print formulas.

Project Purpose:

Help visitors to study faster by giving everything in one place
Allow everyone to study science freely
Provide Quiz to test knowledge

Project Reason:
    I love science (especially Physics) and I was inspired to build a site around one that would be helpful
    to people studying. A place where I can find formulas and explanations easily crossed my mind and I used
    AI to help develop my idea better. This subject was the most appealing and relevant for this assignment.
