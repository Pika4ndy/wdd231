# Science Student Resource Hub Site Plan

## Website Name

Name: **Eureka**

Reason: Evokes the "I found it" Moment of discovery mostly
used by scientist

## Site Purpose

The site will provide several resources for science students like
formulas, cheat sheets, lessons, tests, resaerch papers and interactions with
other students to help them learn, practice and improve their knwoledge about science.

It aims at developing people (and specially youth)'s love for science and promote
science knowledge to everyone.

## Scenarios

- What is the formula for calculating kinetic enrgy again?
- Who founded this field of science or improved it in a singnificant way?
- What are the most recent discoveries in Chemistry?
- I am learning for an exam, what are the every thing I should know about Quantum Mechanics?
- What are random science facts to know for impressing my friends?
